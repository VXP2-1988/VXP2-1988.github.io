Well, it's been awhile since the last post. It's been a rough month due to work and personal life. But, when are things ever easy. I'd be too powerful if anything ever went according to plan. Regardless, classes start again in 31 days and it's time to start thinking about the next hiring cycle. It's not an error I intend to repeat, especially with graduation so close. 

Most of my previous difficulties stem from social anxiety and unfamiliarity with the interview process. I've since started working my way through the Grind75 list and other interview prep materials. Being able to visualize myself going through the process is calming and motivational.

This list was taken from the excellent [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview-questions/). 

### Most commonly asked behavioral questions across top tech companies

~~Why do you want to work for X company?~~

~~Why do you want to leave your current/last company?~~

What are you looking for in your next role?
**I'd like a role that blends physicality and travel with technical problems. I want to help make people's lives better and more secure. I'd like to work on a small team with well-defined roles and responsibilities.**

Tell me about a time when you had a conflict with a co-worker.
**In my software engineering class we had a group project with 6 members. One member was responsible for creating html templates for a particular website view, but did it badly. I ended up having to recreate and extend most of his work. This caused some conflict between us because I was frustrated that his work was incomplete and unusable. Generally people always have a reason for behaving in a certain way, and understanding those reasons smoothes things over considerably.**

**I've also experienced conflict when it comes down to implementation. Sometimes I have butted heads with other coworkers when they have different ideas of how a task should be done, particularly if the other coworker is similarly opinionated. This has taught me the importance of preemptive communication and flexibility.**

Tell me about a time in which you had a conflict and needed to influence somebody else.
**Once I wanted to install a switch and run the cable in a certain way, and the coworker was reluctant to do it my way. Explaining my reasoning led to us being able to agree on a method that worked for us.**

What project are you currently working on?
**I'm currently working on a web app to store and manage invoices for a small company.**

What is the most challenging aspect of your current project?
**Communication, definitely. My client is very busy and doesn't have much time to discuss the project. Their current system is very old and deeply ingrained. This has meant that updating their system has been difficult, and I've largely been given completely free reign on implementation, which is both good and bad.**

What was the most difficult bug that you fixed in the past 6 months?
**Probably diagnosing the cause of slowdowns in my app. Everything was functioning correctly but there was a 1-3 second hang after an invoice was created. It took quite a bit of dissection until I could figure out which element was causing the slowdown, since there was no error code or stack trace to guide me.**

How do you tackle challenges? Name a difficult challenge you faced while working on a project, how you overcame it, and what you learned.
**It's always challenging to learn a new way of doing something, especially alone. You can't know what you don't know. A difficult challenge I faced was implementing testing for my app. I wasn't sure how to test it under load and multiple users, since I was using a hobby account and hadn't gone larger scale before. I overcame this by reading in detail about the capabilities of the server I was using, and implementing multiple checks to make sure that each stage would notify me if something went wrong.**

What are you excited about?
**I'm excited about security and learning more. It seems like it gets easier to do the things I enjoy doing the more I learn, which motivates me to progress until I've reached a stage I'm comfortable at.**

What frustrates you?
**Lack of commmunication and unclear boundaries. I'm deeply frustrated when I feel like I'm wasting time and can't get satisfactory answers.**
