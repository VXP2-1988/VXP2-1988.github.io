*Taking turns, each teacher spoke a word from the text into the ear of her charge and we repeated the word. Then a kind of interrogation ensued, in which the teacher would ask questions related to that word’s possible meanings and associations, always ending by repeating the word. Our only response was to say the word again with whatever feeling the question had elicited.*

*In The Companies She Keeps, Helen Epstein gives an example of Linklater “dropping in” an actor, using Sonnet 29. Kristin advises, “Let the word fall into you: let your mouth feel it, let your middle feel it. Let it play on you. What is the word saying to you?” “Disgrace. When were you last in disgrace? Disgrace. With whom? Disgrace. Where does it sit in your body? Disgrace” (Linklater 110).*

Let’s begin.
A blood-black nothingness begins to spin. Begins to spin. 
You are standing on the edge of the land as it meets the sea.
You are standing on the shore before a body of water.
You are standing on cold sand with the ocean lapping at your feet.
Your mind isn’t your own but you exert what control you can.
Your mind isn’t your own but you demand the control you are owed.
Your mind isn’t your own but you are alive.
You are.
I am. I am. I think therefore I am. 

Strength.
Do you feel strong? Strength.
Does your body feel strong? Strength
What about your back and arms? Are they strong? Strength.
What is Strength? Strength.
Is your mind strong? Strength.
Are you resistant to damage? Strength.
Can you exert your will? Strength.
How strong is your will? Strength.
What do you do to build your strength? Strength.
What do you do to maintain strength? Strength.
How do you protect your strength? Strength.
Are you strong enough to survive? Strength.
Are you strong enough to thrive? Strength.
Are you strong enough to protect those you love? Strength.
Can you keep going? Strength.
Even when you grow tired? Strength.

We’re going to go on.

Purpose.
What is your purpose? Purpose. 
Have you found a purpose? Purpose.
Do you need a purpose? Purpose.
What are you without a purpose? Purpose.
What is your role? Purpose.
What are your responsibilities? Purpose.
Do you feel lost? Purpose.
With nothing to live for? Purpose.
Are you looking for a purpose? Purpose.
Do you have any plans? Purpose.
Are you striving for anything? Purpose.
What is your goal? Purpose.
Why are you alive? Purpose.

Determination.
Are you filled with determination? Determination.
Are you determined? Determination.
Do you decide your own path? Determination.
Are you focused? Determination.
Are you following a path? Determination.
Are you living the way you want to? Determination.
Are you making decisions? Determination.
Are you making judgements? Determination.
Based on the data available to you? Determination.
Do you govern yourself? Determination.
Are you making decisions? Determination.
What is self-determination theory? Determination.
Are you autonomous? Determination.
Are you competent? Determination.
Are you connected to anything else? Determination.
What are the motivations for your actions? Determination.

Perspective.
How do you see yourself? Perspective.
Can you step back? Perspective.
What about the big picture? Perspective.
What do you look like? Perspective.
What are your successes? Perspective.
What are your failures? Perspective.
Do you perceive yourself accurately? Perspective.
Do you perceive yourself compassionately? Perspective.
Are you repeating yourself? Perspective.
Have you felt this before? Perspective.
Where are you in the cycle? Perspective.
Do you believe This Will Pass? Perspective.
What comes afterward? Perspective. 
Can you see a way out? Perspective.
Do you need a way out? Perspective.
Are you happy? Perspective.
Could you be happy? Perspective.

Awe. 
Do you remember the last time you felt awe? Awe.
What makes you feel awe? Awe. 
The sight of a sunrise? Awe.
A storm splitting the sky open? Awe.
Pale lightning flashing across open water? Awe.
The sound of the forest whispering in a strong wind? Awe. 
Does it get stale eventually? Awe. 
Do you miss it when it’s gone? Awe. 
Is this what you live for? Awe.
Do you feel lost without it? Awe. 
What about your dreams at night? Awe.
Do they make you feel awe? Awe.
The ones you try to remember? Awe.
The ones with water-vomit, bones, and endless desert? Awe.
You dreamed about a staircase reaching up into the clouds. Awe.
Your limbs turned to desiccated black claws as you climbed. Awe.
Your ribcage became as fragile as a bird’s. Awe.
You were reaching towards heaven. Awe.
Your companions fell away or broke on the stairs. Awe.
You woke up on a couch under an old polyfill sleeping bag. Awe.
Do you miss that? Awe.
Do you need that? Awe.
You tried to go back by falling asleep again. Awe.
What would you give to experience that sense of significance? Awe.
Would you give your life? Awe. 
Why do you hate waking up? Awe. 
Do you miss your fantasies? Awe.
Is there anything to love down here in the dirt? Awe.
Do you miss your faith? Awe.
Did it give you something to live for? Awe.
Some reason to love yourself and others? Awe. 
Do you really believe in anything now? Awe.
Do you live in fear? Awe.
What will eat you when you die? Awe.
Do you want to be destroyed? Awe. 
Is there any beauty in it? Awe.

Alone.
