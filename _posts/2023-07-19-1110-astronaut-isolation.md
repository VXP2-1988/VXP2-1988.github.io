Posted March 21, 2020; Duration 2:06. Chris Hadfield's tips to productive isolation.

* Understand the risk. Go to a credible source and define the risks in detail.
* Choose goals. What do you want to get done today, this week, or this month?
* Evaluate constraints. Who's commanding you, what are your financial resources, what are your obligations?
* Take action. Do something, even if it's not everything you want to do.
