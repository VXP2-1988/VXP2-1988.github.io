*A blood-black nothingness began to spin*
*A system of cells interlinked within*
*Cells interlinked within cells interlinked*
*Within one stem. And dreadfully distinct*
*Against the dark, a tall white fountain played.*

A system of cells interlinked
Within cells interlinked
Within cells interlinked
Within cells interlinked
Within one stem

System.
Are you part of a system?
What system are you a part of?
Why are you a part of that system?
How many different systems?
Where are you in the system?
What roles do you occupy?
Do you like being a part of the system?
How do you escape the system?
Do you want to?
Do you need to?
Can you?
Does the system sustain you?
Do you exist apart from it?
Do you have anything without it?
Does the system listen to you?
Can you change the system?
Does the system change you?
Do you worship the system?
Do you vilify it?
Is it using you?
Digesting you?
Spitting you out?
Is the system real or a fantasy?
Does it matter?

We're going to go on.

Cells.
Where is the boundary of the cell?
Are the walls concrete, lipid, or mental?
Why are you in the cell?
Do you deserve to be in here?
Do people want you in here?
You are transferred to another cell.
Another bounded box.
Is it any different?
Is this one larger, smaller, warmer, more comfortable?
A cell is a cell is a cell.
Does it matter which one you're in?
Can you leave?
Can the system exist without the cells?
Without the little boxes it uses to build its walls?
Millions on billions of little boxes.
One on top of the other.
Does it matter what's inside them when there are so many?
When what they build together is so grand?
Is the wall of your cell made of billions of little cells?
You knock on the wall and listen for the call of tiny voices.
You hear them but the voices are coming from inside you.
Millions on billions of little boxes.
All the same.
Because if one decided to change and grow it'd be called cancer.

We have to go on.

Interlinked.
