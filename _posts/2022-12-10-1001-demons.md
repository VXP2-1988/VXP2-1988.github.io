So, I was here in the living room pacing back and forth. I'd had a cup of hot ginger tea to help my digestion after dinner. As I paced I watched two demons being born in the darkness behind the wall length windows. They were almost completely invisible, only apparent in the trailing reflection of my own pacing body. 

I feel like I should clarify what a demon looks like. It doesn’t look like anything. I think we’re all past the point where we fear men with goat legs and horns. A demon is the trailing end of the tail of something that rips into reality. It’s not that we can’t see the creature, it’s that there’s really nothing to see. That search for patterns that aren’t there get into your head like a commercial jingle and it doesn’t always get out.

But anyway, I was watching a couple being born behind the blackness and plate glass. They were still people: a man and a woman. A pair of people. I don’t know their histories, they might not have been bad people. Just patterns waiting to interfere.

They looked at each other as they walked past. Their eyes met, like love at first sight but all wrong. That interaction was a spark of something unpleasant, a momentary crack in the smooth continuity of reality. In that moment, the barely perceptible trailing outlines of both individuals were caught in the crack, and when continuity snapped back almost instantaneously, the thin psychic wisps they’d both shed were stuck in that crack.

The wisps writhed and caught at each other. They weren’t even alive enough to be creatures, just a series of shadows. They writhed like a freshly dead and salted muscle would, they caught at each other like burrs on a dogs coat. They tangled in the way that dead things in the sea do: they got stuck, dissolved, and intermingled until you couldn’t tell what you were looking at. Not incomprehensible because of its holiness or devilry, but incomprehensible in the way an old piece of roadkill is incomprehensible. 

This all happened instantly, and the man and woman passed by. They didn’t speak a single word. The thing that had gestated in that instant stuck to both of them, and they went their own separate ways. The demon started life as a shivering silhouette tacked onto the corner of their mouths and eyes. Born on earth to no occult ritual whatsoever, just an interaction at Walmart.

That was a weak demon. They always are when they’re born, but people get around. They have other interactions, open up other momentary cracks, and existing demons can feed on the momentarily shattered peace between ourselves. 

I was raised as a Christian, and in my religion there is an ultimate evil we call Satan. In our holy book, we are taught his name was Lucifer, that he was an angel cast down from heaven in the beginning of time because of his defiance of God.

I’ve tried to sell my soul to him. I’ve drawn runes on my skin, waited at empty crossroads in the dead of night, drawn up contracts, and clutched bones or Petrine crosses. I’ve prayed to God crying so many times that I gave up and prayed to Satan. I can see the afterimages of his demons among us, but never an angel.

But so far he hasn’t answered, and maybe that’s for the best. But it occurs to me that maybe I’m not selling the right way. I have no idea what I’m worth - what is the worth of this soul? It’s an odd fact of our religion that we’re taught that God accepts souls with a long list of conditions, but Satan will straight-up steal or buy them. 

It makes me wonder who actually wants them more.
