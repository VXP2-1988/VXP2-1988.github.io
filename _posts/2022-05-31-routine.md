Switching to a Push/Pull/Legs Split from a Push/Legs/Pull split. Putting Push after Pull on the second weekly cycle may be negatively impacting my progress.

### Cardio
* Rowing Machine
* Running
* Keiser Bike

### Monday -> Push
Heavy shoulders, light chest.
* Overhead Press
* Flat Bench Press
* Incline Bench Press
* Preacher Curl
* Dumbbell Lateral Raise
* Parallel Bar Triceps Dip

### Tuesday -> Pull
Heavy lower back, light upper back.
* Deadlift
* Seated Cable Row
* Lat Pulldown
* Dumbbell Shrug
* Floor Face Pull

### Wednesday -> Core, Legs
Light legs, heavy core.
* Back Squat
* Front Squat
* Calf Raises
* Reverse Crunch
* Seated Ab Circle
* Sledgehammer Swing
* Cable Ab Pulldown

### Thursday -> Push
Heavy chest, light shoulders. 
* Flat Bench Press
* Incline Bench Press
* Overhead Press
* Preacher Curl
* Dumbbell Raise
* Parallel Bar Triceps Dips

### Friday -> Pull
Light lower back, heavy upper back.
* Deadlift
* Barbell Row
* Seated Cable Row
* Lat Pulldown
* Floor Face Pulls
* Dumbbell Shrug

### Saturday -> Legs
Heavy legs, light core.
* Back Squat
* Leg Press
* Calf Raises
* Reverse Crunch
* Seated Ab Circle
* Sledgehammer Swing
* Cable Ab Pulldown

### Sunday
Free day. Rest, stretch, or complete a day that you missed.
